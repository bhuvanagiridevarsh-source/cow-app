**Version:** 2026-09-27

These Terms are an agreement between you and Children of War Project ("CoW", "we"), a student-led initiative based in Monroe Township, New Jersey. By creating an account or using the CoW app, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

## 1. What the app does

The app connects people who want to give away furniture, electronics, appliances, and other items ("donors") with volunteers who pick them up ("volunteers"). CoW turns donated items into money for children affected by war, homelessness, and poverty. CoW's 501(c)(3) status is pending.

## 2. Who can use the app

- You must be at least 13 years old.
- If you are 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must agree to you using the app, and you must give us their name and email.
- To do pickups, you must be at least the minimum pickup age shown in the app (currently 16). When the app asks, volunteers under 18 must bring an adult to the pickup.
- Keep your account to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else used it.

## 3. Zero tolerance for abuse

We do not tolerate abusive, harassing, hateful, sexual, violent, fake, misleading, or unsafe content or behavior. This includes fake listings, inappropriate photos, and harassing other users. We may remove content and suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, without notice. Use **Report** and **Block** in the app to tell us about problems. We review reports as quickly as we can, usually within 24 hours.

## 4. Listing items

- Only list items that you own or have permission to give away, that are legal, and that are safe.
- Do not list weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco or vaping products, medicine, hazardous materials, recalled products, or anything illegal.
- Describe items honestly. The estimated value should be a fair guess of what the item could sell for used.
- Photos should show the item only. Don't include people or anything that shows your house number.
- Your exact address is shared only with the one volunteer you accept, and only until the pickup is finished.
- When you confirm a pickup, the item becomes CoW's to sell or give away. CoW decides how best to use it.

## 5. Picking up items

- Volunteers are not CoW employees. Pickups are voluntary.
- Follow the safety tips in the app: bring a buddy, tell a parent or guardian where you are going, stay at the door, porch, or garage, and leave if anything feels wrong.
- No money changes hands on a pickup.
- Treat donors and their property with respect. Come during the pickup window, or cancel in the app.

## 6. Volunteer hours

Hours are added only after the donor (or a CoW admin) confirms a pickup. CoW decides how many hours a pickup is worth, may change that in the future, and may correct hours that were added by mistake.

## 7. Money donations

Donating money happens on CoW's donation page in your web browser, run by our payment partner Zeffy, not inside the app. That page's own terms apply.

## 8. What we can't promise

We do our best, but the app and every item are provided "as is." CoW does not inspect items. To the extent the law allows, CoW is not responsible for an item's condition or for what happens during a pickup. Please use good judgment and stay safe.

## 9. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Me → Delete account**. We may suspend or remove accounts that break these Terms.

## 10. Changes

We may update these Terms. If a change matters, the app will ask you to accept the new version before you continue.
