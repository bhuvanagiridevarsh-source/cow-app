## Description [4,000]

```
Children of War Project (CoW) is a student-led nonprofit from Monroe Township, New Jersey. We turn things people no longer need into help for children affected by war, homelessness, and poverty.

This app makes it simple.

GIVE WHAT YOU DON'T NEED
• List furniture, electronics, or appliances in about a minute.
• Add a photo, an estimated value, and a pickup time that suits you.
• Your exact address stays private. It is shared only with the one volunteer you accept.

PICK UP AND EARN SERVICE HOURS
• Browse items near you and ask to pick one up.
• See the address only after the donor accepts.
• Earn volunteer hours once the donor confirms the pickup.
• Share a summary of your confirmed hours.

SEE THE IMPACT
• Every confirmed item adds its value to CoW's total impact.
• Watch the lamp glow brighter as we move toward our $25,000 three-year goal.
• Move up CoW's levels, from Volunteer to Change Maker.

BUILT TO BE SAFE
• For ages 13 and up. Teens 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's consent.
• Pickups have a minimum age, and volunteers under 18 bring an adult.
• Safety tips before your first pickup.
• Report and Block on every listing and every pickup.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads. No tracking. No analytics.
• We collect only what's needed to arrange pickups.
• Delete your account anytime, right in the app.

ABOUT COW
CoW began in 2022, when our founder, then 12, collected a decade of loose change to help children in Ukraine. The idea won the Best Social Enterprise Award at the 2024 TiE New Jersey gala. Today CoW is 100% volunteer-run by high-school students, and 100% of net proceeds go to vetted child-welfare organizations chosen by our Board of Trustees & Advisors.

Our slogan: Small change for big change.

Pickups are currently available in New Jersey.
CoW's 501(c)(3) status is pending.

Questions? hello.childrenofwarproject@gmail.com
childrenofwarproject.org
```

## Screenshots

Take them from a real build with the demo accounts, in light mode, with tidy and realistic demo listings (real-looking items, clear photos, no people, no house numbers). Never show a real person's name, email, or address.

**Sizes.** The App Store needs at least one set for the largest iPhone screen (the 6.9-inch size); it scales that set down for smaller phones. Google Play needs 2 to 8 phone screenshots. The app is iPhone-only (no iPad layout), so no iPad set is needed.
