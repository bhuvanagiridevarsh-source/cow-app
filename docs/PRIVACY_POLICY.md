**Version:** 2026-10-10

Children of War Project ("CoW", "we") built this app for students and families. We collect as little as we can. We never sell your information, and the app has **no ads, no analytics, and no tracking**.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your email address (or the email Apple shares, which can be a private relay address), your name, your birth month and year, your ZIP code, and how you want to help.
- **Parent or guardian (ages 13 to 17 only):** their name and email, and when you confirmed their consent.
- **Items you list:** photos, category, name, estimated value, condition, pickup day and time, town, street address, unit, and an optional pickup note.
- **Pickups:** who asked to pick up which item, each step of the pickup and its date, whether an adult is coming, and the volunteer hours earned.
- **Safety:** reports you send and people you block.

## What we don't collect

- We never store your phone's location. If you allow location, it is used only on your phone to work out how far away items are. It is not sent to CoW and not saved.
- Before a photo is uploaded, the app re-saves it, which removes hidden location data. The app checks this and refuses to upload a photo that still has it.
- The camera is used only when you tap to take a photo of an item. The app can only see photos you choose.
- We don't use ads, analytics, or tracking, and we don't sell or share your information for marketing.

## How we use it

We use your information only to run the app: to sign you in, show listings, arrange pickups, keep people safe, count volunteer hours and CoW's total impact, and contact you (or your parent or guardian) about your account if needed.

## Who can see what

- Your email, birthday, ZIP code, and guardian details are private. Only you and CoW admins can see them.
- Other people see only your first name and last initial, and only when you are on a pickup together.
- Listings show the town and an approximate distance. The exact address is shown only to the one volunteer the donor accepts, and only until the pickup is finished.
- Item photos can be seen by signed-in people who can see that listing.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** stores the app's data and photos on servers in the United States. It also sends sign-in codes by email, using our email delivery service.
- **Apple**, if you choose Sign in with Apple.
- **Your phone's maps app (Apple or Google)**, only if a volunteer taps Open in Maps on an accepted pickup. The pickup address is then passed to that app for directions.
- **US Census ZIP code data** (public) gives each listing its approximate location. No lookup of your street address is made.
- **GitHub** hosts the About CoW text the app downloads. Nothing about you is sent to get it.
- **Zeffy** runs CoW's donation page, if you choose to donate money. This happens in your web browser, not in the app.

## What is kept on your phone

So the app still works without a connection, it keeps a copy of some of what you have already seen (your profile, your listings and pickups, nearby items, and CoW's impact total) on your phone for up to 7 days. Exact addresses and your location are never kept this way. Signing out erases this copy.

## Children and teens

The app is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If we learn that a child under 13 signed up, we delete the account. Teens 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's consent. Parents and guardians can email us to see or delete their teen's information.

## Keeping and deleting your information

- You can delete your account at any time: **Me → Delete account**. This immediately deletes your profile, your listings and addresses, your photos, and your blocks.
- We keep a few records without your name: the dollar amounts in CoW's impact total, pickup records so volunteers keep their hours, and reports you sent, so CoW can keep the community safe.
- Otherwise, we keep your information for as long as your account is open.

## Security

Access to data is controlled by strict rules in our database, and data is encrypted while it travels between your phone and our servers. No system is perfect. Please tell us right away if you think your account was misused.

## Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app.
